# 3 to Improve

The session **Analyse Overview** can show up to three evidence-based driving priorities. They summarize the whole eligible session, not only the laps selected for comparison.

Each card shows:

- **Where** an observation occurred: a curated corner name when available, otherwise approximate lap-distance percentage.
- **What happened** and whether evidence comes from one lap or repeats across multiple laps.
- **What to try next**: a practice suggestion tied to the observed pattern.
- **Show evidence**: opens the representative lap and recorded traces around the observed point.

The priorities cover a limited set of recorded patterns: brake drag, downshifts before engine speed falls, abrupt or interrupted throttle application, repeated steering corrections, and front-tire scrub associated with understeer. Suggestions are practice ideas, not proof of cause or guaranteed improvement. The feature does not estimate time gains.

Three is a maximum, not a target. One or two supported findings produce one or two cards. No cards does not mean driving was perfect: the session may lack usable recordings, trustworthy locations, or findings in the supported set. Unsupported telemetry is not treated as evidence that a problem occurred or that driving was correct. Session processing or unavailable evidence can prevent results from appearing; an unavailable result is not a clean result.
