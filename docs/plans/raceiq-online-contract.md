## Ownership and existing contracts

One desktop integration owner controls this contract, browser-safe DTOs in `packages/online/src/contracts.ts`, worker messages, local Hono AppType and migration seams. Cloud owner controls Rust API/SQL migrations, processor, OpenAPI and generated SDK. UI owns components, typed RPC/hooks and locale catalogs. Changes to the boundary update all three plans before dependent work continues. UI/cloud/desktop development can proceed in parallel after interface freeze; real integration is a final dependency.

Existing account/auth/state commands and errors are defined by `packages/online/src/contracts.ts`, `apps/backend/src/routes/` and the generated service contract. Preserve OAuth state/PKCE, loopback/trusted-origin protections, account epochs, revision checks, restrictive barriers, OS-protected credentials, sanitized errors and offline functionality. Extend these types coherently for the single-user worker boundary; do not add compatibility aliases, raw UI fetches, a second account service or success-shaped stubs. SDK baseline is 0.1.13; the v2 contract requires coordinated generation/publication and consumer pinning.

Dashboard semantics are authoritative in `shared/racing/sessions/dashboard.ts`, `server/db/dashboard-queries.ts`, `server/db/dashboard-summary-queries.ts` and `client/src/components/home/HomePageContainer.tsx`. Reuse those calculations, adding driver attribution to their input population. Current headline time uses `drivenSeconds` from positive lap times; session-type time uses separate duration evidence. Favourites rank eligible accumulated seconds, then laps, then stable game/entity identity. Do not replace this with lap-count-first favourites, wall-clock session totals or invented time-category equalities.

## Confirmed product rules

1. Support ACC, AC Evo, F1 2025, iRacing and LMU recording formats. Preserve existing F1 World native car `41` recording/competition eligibility; do not expand public/global/clan competition to other F1 cars. Local F1 use and aggregate statistics remain available for other F1 cars. App submits only the fastest valid wholly self-driven eligible lap per game/track/car. All allowed formats remain release requirements.
2. App never reserves/transfers Forza recordings/setups. Server rejects explicit Forza at admission and rejects inferred/mislabeled/legacy Forza before publication. Aggregate Forza statistics may sync.
3. Dedicated Bun worker owns upload scanning, recording I/O, codec/F1 work, payload preparation, reservations, transfer and retry. Effect PersistedQueue with SQLite owns generic durable delivery; business ledgers own identity, receipts, corrections and history.
4. Settings → Upload is the single read-only queue/status/history surface, with automatic retries. Move queue management out of General rather than duplicate it.
5. Support one local user per installation. Multiple local accounts/profiles, a profile picker, account switching, per-profile libraries and account-replacement flows are out of scope. Offline use and sign-out preserve local activity.
6. First Discord sign-in claims existing unassigned owned records, excluding borrowed community imports. Original cloud ownership is immutable; signing out or presenting a different account must not reassign already claimed history or queued work. Retain the existing single-account authentication flow, not a multi-account credential store.
7. In-game driver identity is independent of the installation user's cloud login and car identity. Verified native evidence or explicit per-game identity binding establishes Self; positive mismatch is Other; uncertain identity is Unknown. Do not match Discord/display names automatically.
8. Shared-car sessions retain stint attribution. Only wholly self-driven laps are personal completed laps or upload candidates. Measurable time is split on actual driver boundaries; personal headline/profile totals exclude Other/Unknown. They remain separate local session context.
9. Local dashboard/profile calculation remains local, with existing period views/counting/favourites/coverage. Online profile is public, account-wide and all-time only; no dated activity-history upload, period filters or daily-history API.
10. Online merges uniquely identified original activity across devices, not device snapshot totals. Copies/imports count once. Unprovable legacy identity remains local and is held out of Online totals with incomplete-coverage feedback.
11. Synced lifetime contributions survive local deletion. Reprocessing replaces the same contribution, including decreases; it does not create new activity. Local dashboard and Online lifetime totals may therefore differ legitimately.
12. Profile sync starts automatically after authentication and explicit acceptance of current policies. No separate profile-sharing toggle. It is independent of public recording/setup preferences; private recording users still sync eligible aggregates after consent.
13. Public recording sharing and optional setup sharing remain separate explicit default-off choices. F1 embedded setup telemetry is removed for every outgoing recording regardless of attachment preference; preserve local originals.
14. Turning public lap sharing off blocks future recording/setup transfers, including retries/backfill; it does not withdraw existing published laps. Sign-out blocks remote actions without deleting local history or stopping offline capture.
15. Changed Terms/Privacy versions pause both recording and aggregate sync until explicit re-acceptance. Cloud enforcement must match; continued-use acceptance is not this release's behavior.
16. Every lap, including invalid/mixed/unknown/local-only laps, has a globally unique persistent GUID. Storage, reprocessing, split/downsample, exports/imports and downloads preserve original identity. A downloaded lap never becomes the downloader's original or upload candidate.
17. Track Details → Laps integrates real Community times and Open Replay for the exact selected lap. Both require sign-in, current consent and effective service-confirmed public recording sharing; no minimum successful upload/publication. Profile-statistics sync alone never unlocks Community access.
18. Source legal documents disclose that community laps/times/replays may be inaccurate, misattributed or tampered. Validation/moderation/removal are reasonable best-effort safeguards, not a guarantee that all offending content is detected/removed or that GUID/validation/publication proves authenticity.

## Global lap GUID and recording provenance

- Use canonical UUID-format lap GUIDs, generated once at first durable lap publication (UUIDv7 for newly created laps). GUID is distinct from local SQLite integer IDs, original activity identity, account ID, upload request key and remote job ID. All laps receive it, not only valid/uploaded laps; do not use a per-installation integer or filename as global identity.
- Persist GUID with the lap and portable artifact/provenance metadata before admission. Preserve it through native-source publication, split/downsample, supported export/import/reprocessing and website/app downloads; native game packet bodies need not be rewritten. Import reuses the original GUID/attribution, not a fresh personal lap. Multiple representations reference one original, not multiple independent GUIDs.
- Backfill legacy laps with a durable reconciliation map. Borrowed cloud records resolve their service-authoritative original GUID; never mint a new personal GUID to bypass missing provenance. Ambiguous duplicate/derivative mappings remain held. Reprocessing preserves a known original GUID; genuine split/merge changes require explicit derivation mapping and cannot silently recycle/remint identity to inflate counts.
- New recording reservation/submit requires `lapGuid`, registered binding ID/revision, `originalActivityId`, `sourceRevision`, `sourceKind:'native_capture'|'verified_portable'` and normal idempotency key. Source identifiers are immutable claims, not ownership certificates; `ownerUserId` is local-only and never submitted as authority. Server obtains owner from verified token, independently validates the logical lap/driver/native content, and binds original GUID through successful authoritative validation. Public/import DTOs retain original GUID/author and genuinely known provenance; caller author strings do not establish ownership.
- Keep unique original-GUID/owner and server-derived recording-identity indexes independent of upload jobs, archive versions and normal payload cleanup. Same owner/same original resolves existing receipt instead of publishing/counting again. Another account claiming known GUID or proven equivalent recording receives 409 `LAP_OWNER_CONFLICT`, no new publication/profile credit; do not disclose the existing owner's private identity in errors.
- Account deletion is distinct from local recording cleanup: remove identifying account links/contributions as required by reviewed deletion policy. If original-lap anti-reassignment provenance is lawfully retained, use the existing unlinked non-authenticatable participant model, not a retained original-account mapping; an anonymized original must not become claimable by another account. Review GUID/fingerprint retention and reidentification risk explicitly before release.
- Server computes a versioned canonical recording fingerprint from validated original logical-lap native evidence, not client hash, GUID, filename, transport compression or claimed author. Known copied content remains detected after GUID substitution and lossless supported wrapper/repackaging changes. Preserve enough native timing/source evidence to distinguish genuinely different laps; never fingerprint only lap time/car/track or use fuzzy similarity as ownership proof. Golden round-trip fixtures must establish each game's supported equivalence relation before claiming protection.
- A GUID is an identifier, not a signature or proof that a human drove a recording. Do not claim protection from arbitrarily altered/synthetic unsigned telemetry. Unknown source/provenance or conflicting identity is rejected/held, never accepted merely because a new GUID was supplied. Existing server-authoritative downloaded provenance and canonical-content comparison enforce the specific known-download reupload prohibition.
- Extend the existing single-lap import manifest to the next schema version with required `lap.guid` (v3 after current v2). Update generation, SDK/schema distribution, desktop importer, website handoff and metadata-only archive migration together. Preserve bytes, original author, timing/provenance and one-lap registration; no re-detection just to add GUID. Release compatible consumer before activating the new manifest; no compatibility alias that reauthors v2 imports.
- Manifest v3 may include optional `provenance={originalActivityId:string|null,sourceRevision:string|null,sourceClock:{domain:string,startMs:number,endMs:number}|null,driverAttributionStints:DriverAttributionStint[]|null}` using the canonical native attribution fields. Include only genuinely known validated/stored source metadata; unavailable values remain absent/null, never inferred from a cloud row/date/name. This one-lap provenance is not a complete original-activity membership commitment. Preserve it on import without turning Community content into personal activity; author ownership remains `author.userId`. E17/E18 verify preservation and that missing optional metadata never becomes fabricated proof.


## Driver/profile and attribution model

- The installation has one local user, independent of auth state. Keep the existing single-account credential lifecycle. Persist only the ownership/binding metadata necessary to prevent reassignment; do not introduce local profile lists, selection generations, per-profile stores or an account-replacement flow. Anonymous pre-first-sign-in owned history remains unassigned until claimed.
- A session retains immutable original ownership plus time-varying in-game driver attribution. A game swap does not change the installation user or log into another account. Other is context, not automatic credit to another account.
- Record source-native driver facts, binding revision and evidence freshness at capture/publication boundaries; adapters define trustworthy native IDs and swap boundaries per game. Binding is explicit when a native source does not establish the local person. Missing/stale/conflicting identity becomes Unknown; do not guess a swap boundary across a gap.
- Preserve attribution and original activity identity through published artifacts, exports/imports and reprocessing. A legacy `ownership='mine'` row or first-account binding proves local record ownership, not that every in-game stint was self-driven.
- A personal lap requires Self evidence over its entire logical lap interval. Mixed/Unknown laps remain viewable as shared-session context, excluded from personal completed-lap counts and personal-best admission. Validity remains an additional upload prerequisite; invalid but wholly personal laps follow existing dashboard count rules.
- Reuse the original dashboard time basis when attributing time. Do not spread seconds proportionally or replace lap-clock time with wall-clock time. Where trustworthy boundaries cannot allocate the metric, retain Unknown coverage. Session-type duration and lap-time metrics remain distinct; do not assert their totals sum to the same value.
- `Other driver` and `unknown driver` differ from `unknown session type`. A positively self-driven interval can have an unknown race/practice category and still contribute to the correct personal metric with classification coverage. Unknown driver time never contributes as Self.

## Worker/lifecycle boundary

One supervised Online worker owns the active single-user service, writable delivery/business stores and aggregate preparation. Main Hono handlers proxy typed requests; main retains callback HTTP ingress and platform-required browser/keystore operations only. Do not run a second main-thread service singleton/coordinator. Reuse existing compiled startup-worker entry conventions, not its unrelated maintenance tasks.

Messages contain `requestId`, operation, `accountEpoch` where account-scoped, existing mutation revisions and bounded payload. Operations include initialization, wake hints, state/queue/history/profile reads, existing account/auth/consent/preferences, game binding and stop. There is no profile-selection operation or driver generation. Reply with DTO or stable sanitized error. Only control metadata/status and necessary ephemeral callback/secret responses cross main; recording/setup buffers do not. Credentials never reach UI, durable messages, logs or stats contributions.

Restrictive changes install a durable barrier before acknowledgment. Main invalidates stale account/revision responses immediately; worker rechecks matching token/account, immutable original owner, accepted current policy and operation-specific preferences immediately before actual SDK transport. Preparation must yield between bounded chunks so queued restrictions can take effect before a new send. Profile sync requires auth/current consent, not public recording visibility. Queued activity permanently retains its owning account.

Authentication does not switch local users, including during capture. First-account claiming is transactional/idempotent; failed/cancelled/stale callbacks cannot rebind history. A different authenticated account cannot overwrite an already claimed original owner or inherit pending work. Do not add account switching, deferred profile activation or a replacement-account flow.

Wake events are hints; durable publication/generation scans recover missed notifications. Queue entries carry stable job references, never credentials or bytes. Pinned Effect 4.0.4 handles locks/heartbeats/generic retry; ledgers preserve immutable payload identity, Retry-After, attempts, acceptance and activity receipts. Shutdown prevents new starts, settles/marks ambiguous work, closes resources and acknowledges with bounded grace; crash recovery reconciles before resend. Unexpected worker death is unavailable, not empty/successful.

## Track Details Community times and Open Replay

Current code inspection: `client/src/components/track/detail/LapManagement.tsx` composes OnlineCommunityLaps for explicit game/track/car, alongside existing legacy community boards. `OnlineCommunityLaps.tsx` fetches Online times and calls startCloudLapImport; `client/src/hooks/cloud-lap-import.tsx` activates `/{game}/sessions/{sessionId}/replay/{lapId}` after registration. This is existing wiring, not fresh runtime proof.

Use one backend-owned entitlement for both protected board reads and app-origin remote replay/import. It requires the connected account, live current-policy acceptance, effective public recording visibility, confirmed preference persistence and no restrictive barrier. Neither aggregate-only profile sync, cached connection display nor historical published laps substitute for current sharing. A newly authorized public user can access before their first upload validates.

Apply entitlement before board fetch, availability/HEAD, download, retry, local registration and Community-triggered cached-import reuse/activation. Direct app routes, retries and native/website deep-link activation cannot bypass it. Recheck account epoch/preference revision after asynchronous work; sign-out/private/policy changes remove stale rows/actions and prevent new network starts, registration or automatic navigation. Bytes already received cannot be recalled; clean unused partials truthfully. Existing independently opened local replays remain usable offline; do not turn revocation into deletion of imported content.

Retain public website browsing and unrelated legacy community surfaces; this is the in-app Online integration restriction. Protected cloud leaderboard operations should enforce current account sharing/consent too without making anonymous public website endpoints private. App import controller must not rely on getImportAccessToken merely returning an authenticated token; it needs the same authoritative entitlement/context as leaderboard access.

The row action named Open Replay uses real import availability, validated source origin, exact selected cloud ID and required original lap GUID. Download one-lap manifest/recording, register committed metadata atomically without lap detection/timing recomputation, retain author/borrowed ownership, deduplicate by service origin plus original identity, and navigate to the real local replay. Game/track/car changes and unavailable/pruned archives produce explicit states; do not open a different lap or merge rows across contexts. Temporary auth/service failure preserves only appropriately scoped last-known state, not authorization.

## Lifetime contribution and cloud API target

Extend the existing stats domain/store and `PUT /api/v1/me/stats`; do not keep unversioned device-snapshot replacement as a competing writer. Migrate all current SDK/public/Discord/export/deletion consumers. These are proposed technical operations to implement, not claims about deployed endpoints. All owner writes require verified bearer identity, exact public Origin and exact-current policy acceptance; private/no-store responses. Server takes account identity from token, never body.

`MetricTotals={laps:number,positiveLaps:number,validLaps:number,drivenSeconds:number,validSeconds:number,distanceMeters:number|null,sessionTypeSeconds:{practice:number,qualifying:number,race:number,unknown:number},sessionsWithDuration:number,sessionsWithoutDuration:number}`. Counts are nonnegative safe integers; metric seconds/metres finite nonnegative. Meanings/eligibility/coverage mirror dashboard definitions, with wholly personal lap filtering. `sessionTypeSeconds` is measured session-duration attribution, not a decomposition of drivenSeconds. Null distance is unavailable, not fabricated zero.

Numeric duration fields are sums of included measured/attributed observations, not claims about unobserved duration. When no matching-clock duration is usable, the empty sum is 0, sessionsWithDuration is 0, affected eligible sessions count in sessionsWithoutDuration, and sessionDuration coverage is unavailable. Consumers must render unavailable, never measured zero, for that tagged state; partial coverage displays only included measurements as partial. A genuinely measured zero with complete coverage remains zero. Do not create duration from lap time or assume clocks are equal.

`EntityContribution={gameId:string,nativeId:string,seconds:number,laps:number}` uses the dashboard's eligible favourite contributions, not preselected favourite names. `ActivityContribution={schemaVersion:2,activityId:string,lapGuids:string[],expectedRevision:number|null,contentHash:string,gameId:string,metrics:MetricTotals,cars:EntityContribution[],tracks:EntityContribution[],coverage:ProfileCoverage}`. lapGuids are unique original personal-lap GUIDs behind the contribution, never borrowed/Other laps; server checks known owner and overlapping contributions so a new activityId cannot recount a known lap. `ProfileCoverage={identity:'complete'|'partial',driverAttribution:'complete'|'partial',sessionDuration:'complete'|'partial'|'unavailable',distance:'complete'|'partial'|'unavailable'}` describes included coverage; held counts are separate local/read-model fields. Do not advertise entire-account completeness from these flags alone.

`DriverSummary={metrics:MetricTotals,games:{gameId:string,metrics:MetricTotals,favoriteCar:FavoriteEntity|null,favoriteTrack:FavoriteEntity|null}[]}`, with games sorted by ID. `FavoriteEntity={nativeId:string,name:string,seconds:number,laps:number}`; apply existing dashboard ranking and canonical identity/naming. No activity dates, driver names, hardware IDs, source paths, recordings or setups in aggregate contributions. Forza entities/metrics are permitted.

Profile statistics are account-reported, not recording-ownership certification. Accept eligible local-only/invalid/private-lap GUIDs through the existing contribution operation without requiring a recording upload or a new proof/installation-registration endpoint. Unknown GUIDs may have unbound provenance with no validated owner; contribution claims/receipts are a separate business ledger, not permission to bind recording ownership. Reject known validated foreign originals. Conflicting unproven cross-account claims remain held, never silently treated as validated ownership; a reported claim cannot block later authoritative recording validation. Such validation must quarantine incompatible reported contributions and atomically correct affected summaries/coverage, retaining owner-readable held receipts rather than transferring another account's contribution.

Account-scoped contribution storage must distinguish an invalidated claim from validated original ownership; a weak claim cannot permanently squat the legitimate owner's activity ID. Keep original-GUID deduplication and same-account revision fences. No raw recordings, other-driver identity, hardware IDs or dated history are added to aggregate sync.

Updates use bounded incremental metric/entity/coverage accounting from the previous and replacement contribution, not a parse/rebuild of every lifetime contribution per write. Preserve existing favourite ranking, finite/count bounds and decreases. There is no new arbitrary lifetime activity/storage ceiling; per-request size/rate limits and intrinsic numeric bounds still apply.

Content hash protocol is the Rust stats encoder's versioned typed binary format: domain `raceiq.activity-contribution.v2\0`, fixed schema/field order, little-endian counts and exact finite IEEE-754 doubles, UTF-8 length-prefixed strings and canonical UTF-8 ordering of GUID/entity sets; normalize negative zero without quantizing values. Exclude expectedRevision/contentHash. Publish one compatible SDK encoder/hash helper plus cross-language vectors; do not duplicate JSON-stringify hashes. Verify the exact field order/tags against the source encoder and executable vectors before activation.
