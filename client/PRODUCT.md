# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Sim racers across Forza Motorsport, F1 2025, Assetto Corsa Competizione, and AC Evo. They use RaceIQ in two distinct contexts equally: live, glancing at the telemetry dash while actively driving or streaming a session, and afterward, doing deep post-session review to shave lap time. Both contexts demand fast, glanceable reads of dense data, but the live context tolerates zero friction while the review context rewards depth.

## Product Purpose

RaceIQ ingests real-time UDP telemetry from multiple racing games, stores lap history, and surfaces both live dashboards and AI-powered lap analysis so a driver can find where they're losing time and fix it. Success is a driver walking away from a session with a concrete, corner-specific next action.

## Positioning

The only telemetry tool that unifies multiple racing games under one interface and pairs raw data with AI coaching — instead of a single-game overlay or a spreadsheet of numbers, a driver gets an explanation.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, fast. The voice of an engineer who respects your time: no fluff, no hand-holding tone, straight to the number and what it means. Confidence comes from data density done well, not from decoration.

## Anti-references

Not a generic SaaS product — no gradient hero-metric tiles, no glassmorphism, no cutesy rounded card grids that could belong to any startup. Also not a raw sim-racing overlay (MoTeC-style) — dense and numeric is fine, but never ugly, uncalibrated, or engineer-only-tool grade with no design polish. The bar is data-dense AND well-designed, not one traded for the other.

## Design Principles

- Density with clarity: pack real telemetry data into the frame without resorting to filler cards or hero-metric cliches.
- Serve both contexts: live views prioritize zero-friction glanceability; review views prioritize depth and drill-down.
- Data-dense, never cluttered: precision reads as confidence, not as chaos.
- No decoration without information: every visual element should encode a value or state, not just fill space.
- AI as explanation, not spectacle: analysis output should read as a trusted engineer's note, not a chatbot flourish.
